Sri Bhadrakali Devasthanam- Warangal

The temple is believed to be built in 625 A.D by the King Pulakeshin II of Chalukya dynasty to commemorate his victory over Vengi region of Andhra Desham.

As per the writings on the temple wall. Kakatiya kings later have adopted the temple and considered Goddess Bhadrakali as their “Kula Devatha”.

The temple is known for its Chalukyan style architecture and intricate carvings.
